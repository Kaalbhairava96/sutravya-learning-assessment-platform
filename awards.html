FROM IDEA TO IMPACT
THE QUALINOVA STARTUP ENABLEMENT PROGRAM

We don’t want your idea to end at the award ceremony.

For the top three Hackathon teams, QualiNova aims to create a bridge from a promising concept to a working healthcare product—and from a working product to a sustainable venture.

Selected teams will be supported with a structured development pathway, seed-funding support and practical assistance across the journey. The goal is to help founders validate the problem, build the right solution, test it responsibly and prepare it for real-world adoption.

✦Your idea gets more than applause. It gets a platform to grow.

Recognition & programme notes

BCMR credit-point eligibility and issuance are subject to applicable council requirements, verified participation and the relevant approval process. Workshop attendance certificates and Hackathon completion certificates are issued against their respective participation criteria. Hackathon cash rewards, final rankings and seed-funding support are subject to the organiser’s official terms, team verification, feasibility review, agreed development scope and milestone-based arrangements. Any pilot, clinical, regulatory or market activity will require the appropriate permissions and independent approvals.
